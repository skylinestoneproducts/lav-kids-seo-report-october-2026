Data notes

Both periods are 24 days long: 1–24 October 2026 against 7–30 September 2026.

UK Search Console figures use the country filter United Kingdom. All-country figures have no country filter.

CTR is recalculated from clicks and impressions, so it can differ slightly from Search Console rounding.

The GA4 property time zone is America/Los_Angeles; dates were queried exactly as shown.

GA4 revenue covers all channels and is not automatically attributed to SEO.

Brand queries (searches containing “Lav Kids”) are kept apart from non-brand queries because they reflect existing awareness rather than SEO reach.
